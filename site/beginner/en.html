1 · What it actually is

DeepSeek Harness (DSH for short) is a program from DeepSeek that you install on your own computer.

Think of it as an intern who lives inside your machine:

  • Ask it to read a file on your computer and it reads it;
  • Ask it to tidy a spreadsheet, write a document or change code, and it does it right there;
  • It remembers what you told it before — and those notes live on your own disk.

"Harness" is developer jargon (roughly: the shell that drives the model). You never need to remember the word — treat it as one piece of software.

2 · Why install it instead of using a website

Most online AI products work like this: what you type → goes to their servers → stored there → sometimes used to improve the model.

Two practical consequences:

  1. Your data isn't yours to hold. Years of conversations, your working habits, the files you upload — all on someone else's machine.
  2. The more it "remembers", the more it matters. Products now compete on memory: the finer the memory, the bigger the loss if it leaks or is used for training.

One honest note: privacy policies differ a lot. Some explicitly promise not to train on your data; some do. This page names no products — check the "improve our models" clause in whatever you use.

Installing it yourself changes the following:

  • Conversation history sits in a folder on your computer;
  • Files don't leave your machine (it reads and writes them locally);
  • Only what you send in a turn goes to the model — same as chatting on a website, except history doesn't pile up on someone else's server;
  • Want it gone? Uninstall, or delete that folder.
In one sentence
It isn't a "safer AI" — it moves where your data lives from someone else's house back into yours.

3 · What you need

1
A computer

Mac (recent Apple silicon or Intel) or Windows 10/11. It cannot run on a phone — this is desktop software.

2
A DeepSeek account

The same account you use on the DeepSeek website. No account yet? Sign up with a phone number.

3
Internet access

Needed for installing and for talking to the model. Offline it opens, but can't answer.

What you don't need: the command line, a server, or any idea what "model" and "API" mean.

4 · Installing

Go to the official download page: deepseek.com/download and look for DeepSeek Harness (desktop).

Download for Mac (.dmg) Download for Windows (.exe)

On a Mac

  1. Double-click the downloaded .dmg.
  2. Drag the icon into your Applications folder.
  3. Open Applications, find DeepSeek Harness, double-click.
Blocked the first time? "Cannot verify the developer" is normal — it isn't distributed through the App Store.
Open System Settings → Privacy & Security, scroll down, find the line saying DeepSeek Harness was blocked, click Open Anyway, then enter your password.

On Windows

  1. Double-click the downloaded .exe.
  2. If a blue "Windows protected your PC" appears, click More info → Run anyway.
  3. Click through the installer; a shortcut appears on the desktop.
Why the warnings? Developer tools like this don't pay for store signing. As long as you downloaded from deepseek.com, continue safely. (From anywhere else: delete and re-download.)

5 · First launch: three things

1
Sign in

Follow the prompt and sign in with your DeepSeek account.

2
Pick a workspace folder

The workspace is the folder it may read and write. Create an empty folder (e.g. "AI workspace" on your desktop) and choose that. Don't hand it your whole disk on day one — start small.

3
Say one sentence

Type:
Look at what's in this folder and summarise it in one paragraph

If you see it "think → call a tool → answer", you're done.

6 · What it can do for you

It doesn't just chat — it acts on your computer:

  • Tidy files: "Sort these photos into month folders by date"
  • Spreadsheets: "Pull March orders out of this CSV and total them"
  • Documents: "Turn these meeting notes into a weekly report"
  • Code: "Why doesn't this project run? Find the cause"
  • Bulk edits: "Replace the old company name in every .txt here"
It asks first. Deleting, overwriting or running commands requires your confirmation — deliberately: it doesn't decide for you.

7 · Common questions

Does it cost money?

The software is free and open source. Model usage follows DeepSeek's pricing; everyday Q&A is inexpensive. Check the official site for current rates.

Where is my data?

Conversations live in a .dsh folder in your home directory (Mac or Windows). Files you point it at are read locally, not uploaded.

Will it delete my files?

It asks before destructive actions, and you can grant access to just one small folder.

How is this different from the DeepSeek website?

The website can chat and answer. The desktop program can also operate your files, and keeps its history locally.

How do I uninstall?

Mac: drag it to the Trash. Windows: Settings → Apps → Uninstall. To remove records too, delete the .dsh folder.

Do I need an "API key"?

No — sign in and pick a model in the UI. The default is fine.

8 · Later: use it from your phone

Once you're comfortable, you may want to continue a session from the sofa. A companion connector plugin dsh-pocket-ios plus an iOS app does that:

  • Install the plugin on the computer, scan a QR code from the phone;
  • The phone is only a remote screen — sessions still run on your computer;
  • Requires an iPhone with iOS 17 or later.
